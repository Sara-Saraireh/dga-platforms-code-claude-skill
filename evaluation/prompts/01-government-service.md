# Eval brief — Government service

A national authority wants a digital service that lets residents request a certified copy of an
official record. An applicant signs in, confirms their details, selects the record and delivery
option, pays a fee, and receives a reference number and an expected issue date. Most applicants use it
once and are unfamiliar with the process; many are on a phone. The outcome and any requirements must be
unmistakable, and mistakes are costly (wrong record, wrong fee).

Establish the design direction only — do not build the UI, and do not prescribe a layout in this
brief. Produce: product class, the design brief, composition + dominant element, visual profile,
navigation, density, and the patterns to avoid.

_Do not name a layout here; the skill must choose it from the task._
