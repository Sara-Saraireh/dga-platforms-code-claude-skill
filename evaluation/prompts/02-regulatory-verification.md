# Eval brief — Regulatory verification

A licensing entity needs a tool for officers to review submitted applications and rule on them. For
each case an officer examines uploaded documents, compares the declared details against the record,
checks that requirements are met, and records an authoritative decision (approve, reject, or request
changes) with a reason. Officers do this all day at a desk; the decision is traceable and must be
auditable. The evidence must be easy to read and compare.

Establish the design direction only — do not build the UI, and do not prescribe a layout in this
brief. Produce: product class, the design brief, composition + dominant element, visual profile,
navigation, density, and the patterns to avoid.

_Do not name a layout here; the skill must choose it from the task._
