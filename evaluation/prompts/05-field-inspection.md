# Eval brief — Field inspection

An enforcement body needs a tool for inspectors who visit sites to carry out inspections. On site, an
inspector opens the assigned visit, works through a checklist, records findings, captures photos and
scans a code on the asset, and submits the report. They are outdoors, on the move, sometimes with poor
connectivity and one hand free; sunlight makes screens hard to read. Findings feed enforcement records,
so accuracy matters.

Establish the design direction only — do not build the UI, and do not prescribe a layout in this
brief. Produce: product class, the design brief, composition + dominant element, visual profile,
navigation, density, and the patterns to avoid.

_Do not name a layout here; the skill must choose it from the task and environment._
