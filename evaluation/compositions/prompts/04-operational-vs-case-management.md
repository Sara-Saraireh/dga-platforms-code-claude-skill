# Eval brief — Operational vs case management (distinction)

Two teams at the same agency need tools, and the skill must choose the right structure for each — they
are not the same:

- **Team A (intake desk):** handles a continuous, high volume of incoming requests. They scan the
  queue, assign each request, move it through a few lightweight states (new → in progress → closed),
  and resolve it quickly. Speed and not losing their place matter most; each request is short-lived.
- **Team B (investigations):** handles complex, long-lived cases. Each case accumulates evidence over
  weeks, involves several parties, has a decision history and a timeline, and is understood by reading
  its full context before acting.

For **each** team, select the composition **and a specific variant**, state the dominant element and
structural regions, and explain the cue that decides between operational-workspace and
case-management-split-view.

_Do not prescribe the structure in this brief and do not build UI; the skill must choose the composition
and variant from each team's task._
