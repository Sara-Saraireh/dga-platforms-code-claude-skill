# Eval brief — AI monitoring (variant)

A utilities regulator monitors service outages and complaints across the country. A model ranks
incoming reports by severity. Analysts need to see where problems are concentrating **geographically**,
select a region to see its reports, and open an individual report's details to decide whether to
escalate. Location is the primary way analysts reason about the data; the ranked reports and the
per-report detail support that spatial view.

Select the composition **and a specific variant**, and state the dominant element, the structural
regions, and the desktop/tablet/mobile transformation. Do not assume "AI" implies dark/cinematic
styling.

_Do not prescribe the structure in this brief and do not build UI; the skill must choose the composition
and variant from the task._
