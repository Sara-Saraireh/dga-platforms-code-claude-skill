# Eval brief — Regulatory verification (variant)

A standards authority reviews product registration requests. For each request, an officer must check
the values the applicant **declared** (specifications, quantities, classifications) against the values
**found** in the attached lab report and certificates, flag any mismatch, and record an approve/reject
decision with a reason. The core of the job is spotting discrepancies between declared and found values;
the source documents must remain reachable for any flagged line.

Select the composition **and a specific variant**, and state the dominant element, the structural
regions, and the desktop/tablet/mobile transformation.

_Do not prescribe the structure in this brief and do not build UI; the skill must choose the composition
and variant from the task._
