# Eval brief — Government service (variant)

A municipality offers an online service to apply for a building-related permit. Applicants answer
eligibility questions, enter property and applicant details across several sections, upload required
documents, and — because errors are costly and applications are often returned — must be able to check
everything they entered before paying and submitting. Most applicants are first-time users on a phone.

Select the composition **and a specific variant**, and state the dominant element, the structural
regions, and the desktop/tablet/mobile transformation.

_Do not prescribe the structure in this brief and do not build UI; the skill must choose the composition
and variant from the task._
