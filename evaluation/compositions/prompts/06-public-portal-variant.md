# Eval brief — Public portal (variant)

A national programme wants a public page to report the results of its first year to citizens: what the
programme set out to do, and the outcomes achieved, communicated through a short narrative supported by
a few headline figures (people served, facilities opened, satisfaction). Visitors are the general
public, mostly on phones, who should read and understand the results and then follow a link to learn
more or access related services. The figures illustrate the story; they are not an interactive
dashboard.

Select the composition **and a specific variant**, and state the dominant element, the structural
regions, and the desktop/tablet/mobile transformation.

_Do not prescribe the structure in this brief and do not build UI; the skill must choose the composition
and variant from the task._
