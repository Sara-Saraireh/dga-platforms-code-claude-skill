# Eval brief — Field mobile (variant)

A food-safety authority sends inspectors to restaurants. On site, an inspector works through a fixed
inspection checklist — item by item — recording a result for each, capturing a photo where a violation
is found, and progressing until the checklist is complete and the report is submitted. Inspectors are
on the move, often one-handed, sometimes with poor connectivity, and the checklist can be long.

Select the composition **and a specific variant**, and state the dominant element, the structural
regions, and the desktop/tablet/mobile transformation.

_Do not prescribe the structure in this brief and do not build UI; the skill must choose the composition
and variant from the task and environment._
